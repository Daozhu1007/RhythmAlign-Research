# Owner Capture Checklist (one page — bring this to the arcade)

**Goal:** 3 short sessions, ~5 minutes of recording each. Just follow the
boxes. You do not need to understand the research; recording matters, perfect
execution does not.

## BEFORE RECORDING (once)

- [ ] Use the **same phone** you normally record handcams with
- [ ] Mount the phone **where you normally mount it** for handcam videos
- [ ] Turn OFF (if the phone has these options): noise reduction, wind
      reduction, spatial/3D audio, auto "enhancements"
- [ ] Airplane mode ON (no notification sounds)
- [ ] Choose the **best quality** format the camera/recorder app offers
- [ ] Do NOT edit, trim, or convert anything — original files only

## SESSIONS (do this 3 times — e.g., 3 different days or setups)

Each session = **three recordings, in this order:**

**Recording 1 — QUIET INTERACTION (~3 minutes)**
Music volume OFF or as quiet as the arcade allows. Nobody talks.
- [ ] ~1 min: touch NOTHING (silence/rest — leave this in, it matters!)
- [ ] Weak taps: light finger touches, gentle grazes
- [ ] Normal hits: play a few notes at usual strength
- [ ] Strong hits: firm presses / palm strikes
- [ ] Let rings decay: after some hits, hands off and wait 2–3 s
- [ ] Slow button releases
- [ ] Screen taps (if the cabinet has a touch area)
- [ ] Slider travel both ways + palm friction/drag
- [ ] ~30 s: fast dense play-like actions
- [ ] ~30 s: single isolated taps with silence between

**Recording 2 — PLAYBACK ONLY (~1 minute)**
- [ ] Same phone position, hands OFF the machine entirely
- [ ] Music playing at normal volume (or just the room if music can't play)

**Recording 3 — NORMAL GAMEPLAY (~1 minute)**
- [ ] Play normally, music ON, everything as usual

## BETWEEN SESSIONS

- [ ] Note roughly where the phone was and what changed between sessions
      (position/cabinet/room) — a few words is enough

## AFTER RECORDING (at home)

- [ ] Do NOT edit, trim, convert, or "clean up" anything
- [ ] Copy the ORIGINAL files into the inbox folder you were given
      (`session_01`, `session_02`, `session_03` — one folder per session)
- [ ] Keep the original file names as the phone created them
- [ ] Tell the research side: phone model, app used, and anything unusual
      (auto gain changes, notifications, interruptions)

**That's everything. Thank you — this recording is the most valuable
missing piece of the whole project.**
