### 3. Truth quality

S0 truth tiers (T0–T4) are enforced in the manifest tooling; every pilot asset gets an
explicit tier, plus `SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE` for tooling-only media. Contamination is
flagged, never silently relabeled: QC emits `clipped` / `high_floor` and the protocol
requires `clean_quiet` vs `playback_present_low` vs `contaminated` human assignment
from source evidence. Close/contact channels are proxies/evidence (T0), never
phone-domain stems. Primary representation feasibility is T2-only by construction.

### 4. Synchronization quality

[SYNC_PROTOCOL.md](SYNC_PROTOCOL.md): two visible+audible anchor events (countdown +
clap + tap) per session; `t_target = a + b·t_aux` least-squares fit with leave-one-out
validation (≤ 2 ms for separately recorded devices); clock drift and acoustic
propagation delay kept separate via documented geometry — never "corrected" to force
peak coincidence; separator output is never aligned to truth independently. The
machinery is validated on fixtures (exact fit recovered, contact lead preserved);
the real-session verdict is pending capture.

### 5. Exact mixture construction

[recipes](MIXTURE_RECIPES.json): `y = s + n` in float PCM with every component stored
and hashed; additive gains only, target at recorded gain, **no normalization**;
levels −20/−10/0/+10 dB defined on annotated active target regions with a frozen
floor; overlap cases (weak target + loud speech, onset+onset, friction+music,
dense+music, target+similar impact) and composite nuisance are in the panel template;
gains frozen before any model output. The 12-case fixture panel validated the whole
path (stored-float32 sum identity ≤ 2.4e-7; measured-vs-requested levels within 0.1 dB).
The real panel (12–24 cases from real T1 segments) is generated by the same
`05_build_mixtures.py` once capture exists.

### 6. CLAPSep representation audit

Inspected (not assumed) from vendored source, hashes pinned in
[PILOT_MANIFEST.json](PILOT_MANIFEST.json): STFT n_fft 1024 / hop 320 / periodic hann /
center / reflect at 32 kHz (torchlibrosa); decoder head `sigmoid` bounded **real
magnitude mask**, `phase: False`; reconstruction `relu(mag·M)` with mixture phase,
length-preserving torchlibrosa ISTFT (window² overlap-add normalization, edge trim).
Structural consequences: linearity in Y (exact per-path decomposition available);
binwise amplitude ceiling `|Ŝ_f| ≤ |Y_f|` under mixture phase — destructive
interference is unrepresentable at those bins. Full audit:
[REPRESENTATION_DIAGNOSTICS.md](REPRESENTATION_DIAGNOSTICS.md).

### 7. Bounded-mask oracle results

`BOUNDED_REAL_MASK_ORACLE` `clip(Re(S·conj(Y))/(|Y|²+ε),0,1)` and
`OPTIMIZED_BOUNDED_MASK_ORACLE` (direct waveform-loss optimization of M ∈ [0,1];
deterministic init; fixed budget; finite-difference-validated adjoint) are implemented
on the exact convention and pass all six Part-15 synthetic cases (Y=S; S=0; separate TF
regions → 52.9 dB; destructive interference → bounded gain −14.0 dB while complex
recovers at 155 dB; continuous tone no-chopping; impulse timing/amplitude). Convention
verified against the actual torchlibrosa modules in the R2 torch venv (rel err ≤ 1.4e-6,
`logs/stft_equivalence_check.json`). **Real-T2 bounded-mask results: none exist yet**
(SYNTHETIC_FIXTURE exercise numbers in REPRESENTATION_RESULTS.json carry no real-acoustic
meaning).

### 8. Complex/oracle diagnostics

`COMPLEX_RATIO_ORACLE` (unbounded complex ratio) and `TRUE_TARGET_STFT_ROUNDTRIP` are
implemented and validated; on the destructive-interference unit case the complex oracle
recovers the target exactly where the bounded family is amplitude-limited — the exact
diagnostic separation the stage requires between "representation limitation" and
"data difficulty". These are diagnostics, not models and not deployable separators.

### 10. Failure cases

Failure taxonomy and detection are wired, with no real failures to report yet: mixture
sum identity, sync adequacy, clipping/contamination flags, oracle unit tests, aliasing
checks, normalization prohibition, and no-post-hoc-tuning are enforced as stop
conditions in `91_run_synthetic_pipeline.py` / `09_pilot_manifest.py` (all currently
green on fixture artifacts). The known-by-construction failure mode of the bounded
family (amplitude ceiling under destructive interference) is demonstrated and
quantified by the unit case in §7/§8.
